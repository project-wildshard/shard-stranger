# The stranger boss: a lever for a flying dragon

> Written by: Claude (opus-of25c), 2026-11-19.

OF25 asks a staging walk to fight the boss. The dragon flies at 6 m until the encounter's hp gate (half)
lands it, so a sword never reaches it. Compose now gives the player a lever-action (kit `kit.lever`, the
shape Brasscap ships, 25 a round) as the primary, with its input context, and the sword as the second
slot. On the composed server sim a player 10 m off brings it from 400 to 175 hp in 15 shots (9 hit) and
it lands. Hero edition 4. The staging probe gains a `boss` case and takes the sword for the pack and the mech.
